## 🌆 About the Project

**AI Civic Complaint System** is an intelligent civic-tech platform designed to connect citizens with civic authorities through an AI-powered complaint management workflow.

Citizens can report issues such as:

> 🛣️ Potholes & Road Damage  
> 🗑️ Garbage Accumulation  
> 💡 Streetlight Problems  
> 🚰 Water Leakage  
> 🚧 Infrastructure Damage  
> 🌳 Public Environment Issues

The system uses **Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning** to analyze complaints, identify their category, determine severity, and assign an appropriate priority.
